# BLTZ
### The Structured Identity Layer for Athletes

BLTZ is building the modern digital locker — a verified, AI-powered identity platform for athletes.

We are creating the infrastructure layer where athletes can own their career data, publish structured public profiles, and unlock future monetization opportunities.

---

# 🏈 The Problem

Athletes lack a unified system to:

- Own and control their career narrative  
- Showcase verified achievements in a structured format  
- Build long-term digital equity  
- Monetize highlights and performance data  
- Transition their identity beyond sport  

Current solutions are fragmented:
- Social media is noisy and unstructured  
- Recruiting platforms are closed ecosystems  
- Legacy systems do not prioritize athlete ownership  

There is no standardized digital identity layer for athletes.

---

# 🚀 The Solution

BLTZ provides:

### 1️⃣ Verified Handle-Based Profiles  
Athletes claim a permanent identity:  
`bltz.me/[handle]`

### 2️⃣ AI-Enriched Career Data  
Automated enrichment of:
- Biography  
- Awards  
- Highlights  
- Structured career history  

### 3️⃣ Modular Digital Lockers  
Customizable content blocks that allow athletes to build professional-grade profiles.

### 4️⃣ Secure Infrastructure  
Role-based access control and structured database architecture powered by Supabase.

### 5️⃣ Foundational Analytics  
Athletes gain insight into visibility and engagement.

BLTZ transforms athlete identity from social presence to structured digital asset.
